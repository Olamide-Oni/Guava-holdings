Introducing Guava FreeZone Estate, A Project developed by Guava Holdings’ subsidiary in Ibeju-Lekki!

Whether you're looking to build your family home or invest in a future hotspot, the newly launched Guava FreeZone City located in Ibeju-Lekki is for you!
