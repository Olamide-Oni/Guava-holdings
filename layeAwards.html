Guava Holdings MD/CEO Receives Prestigious Real Estate Award at the Lagos Young Entreprenuership Awards (LAYE)

Guava Holdings hit another milestone as our MD/CEO, Mr Chukwuemeka Eke, was honoured with the Real Estate & Property Development Entrepreneur of the Year Award at this year's edition of the Lagos Young Entrepreneurship Awards (LAYE), held on Saturday, October 19th. This recognition highlights not just his personal dedication but also Guava Holdings' collective effort and commitment to reshaping the Lagos real estate market.


The LAYE Awards are designed to celebrate vibrant, driven entrepreneurs who are transforming industries and creating jobs across Lagos. In recognising Mr. Eke, the awards also acknowledged Guava Holdings' unwavering commitment to delivering exceptional quality and innovative property solutions for Lagosians.


Though Guava Holdings is still a growing company, our ambition is clear: to set a new standard in Lagos real estate by offering a variety of premium properties that cater to both local residents and Nigerians in the diaspora.


Mr. Eke’s leadership has seen Guava Holdings carve out a niche in the bustling Lagos property market, standing out for our client-centric approach and variety of properties that blend luxury with comfort. This award is more than just recognition—it’s proof that Guava Holdings is on the path to becoming a significant player in the industry.


As we celebrate this achievement, we are more determined than ever to continue delivering value, one property at a time.
